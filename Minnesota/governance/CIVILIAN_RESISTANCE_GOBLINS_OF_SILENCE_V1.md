# Civilian Resistance — The Goblins of Silence v1

## Status

`DRAFT_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW`

## Definition

Civilian Resistance is the lawful, nonviolent public practice of preserving facts, authored opinions, dissent, timelines, evidence, and unmet obligations when institutions depend on silence, fragmentation, delay, or procedural opacity.

It does not create police power, emergency authority, military authority, or a right to threaten, coerce, harass, trespass, sabotage, or impersonate officials.

## The Goblins of Silence

“The Goblins of Silence” is the satirical name for institutional failure patterns that survive because nobody can easily see or replay them.

```text
NO RECORD
NO RESPONSE
NO VERSION HISTORY
NO NAMED AUTHORITY
NO DEADLINE TRACE
NO MINORITY REPORT
NO RECEIPT
```

A Goblin of Silence is not a person-label or criminal accusation. It is a documented pattern in a process, record system, or organization.

## Women’s opinion rule

A woman’s participation is not fully recorded by:

```text
YES
NO
ABSENT
SILENT
```

The record must preserve her authored opinion, evidence, objections, alternatives, conditions, unanswered questions, and the organization’s substantive response.

```text
VOTE != COMPLETE OPINION
SILENCE != CONSENT
ABSENCE != AGREEMENT
```

## Goblin detector

A process enters review when one or more conditions appear:

```text
DOCUMENT EXISTS BUT CHANGE IS UNEXPLAINED
DECISION EXISTS BUT AUTHORITY IS UNNAMED
VOTE EXISTS BUT OPINIONS ARE MISSING
FEEDBACK WAS RECEIVED BUT RESPONSE IS GENERIC
DEADLINE EXISTS BUT CALCULATION IS HIDDEN
NEW PDF REPLACED OLD PDF WITHOUT VERSION NOTICE
PUBLIC CLAIM EXISTS WITHOUT SOURCE BYTES
MINORITY REPORT WAS OMITTED
WOMEN’S CONCERNS WERE REDUCED TO TURNOUT OR SILENCE
```

Detection creates a review task, not a finding of guilt or misconduct.
