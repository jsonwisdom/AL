# Minority Report: Mom Edition — Minnesota v1

## Working slogan

**When the grid goes down, Moms go nuclear.**

“Nuclear” is rhetorical: maximum lawful civic energy, documentation, mutual aid, and accountability. This framework does not authorize weapons, vigilantism, coercion, impersonation of officials, or emergency-command powers.

## Mission

Create county-level mobile civilian units that preserve local memory, surface women’s observations and dissent, support vulnerable residents, and keep public decisions inspectable during communications outages, infrastructure failures, severe weather, emergency closures, or institutional silence.

## Canonical name

```text
MOMS
Mobile Oversight and Mutual-Support Units
```

Optional field nickname:

```text
KAREN
Knowledge, Accountability, Resilience, and Emergency Network
```

The nickname is satire. The operating doctrine is serious.

## Core premise

A county is not resilient merely because officials retain communications. It is resilient when ordinary people can still:

- find one another;
- preserve observations;
- distinguish fact from rumor;
- identify unmet needs;
- document official actions and failures;
- route emergencies to lawful responders;
- preserve minority opinions and unresolved objections;
- reconstruct the timeline after systems return.

No title creates governmental authority.

## Mobile squad function

A mobile squad is a small civilian team that can move between neighborhoods, shelters, libraries, public meetings, charging sites, cooling or warming centers, and other lawful public-access points.

Permitted functions:

- check welfare with consent;
- distribute verified public information;
- collect needs reports;
- document service interruptions;
- preserve written and audio statements with consent;
- record women’s authored opinions, objections, alternatives, and unanswered questions;
- provide paper forms when digital systems fail;
- timestamp and hash collected artifacts after connectivity returns;
- route emergencies to 911 or the appropriate lawful service;
- publish only after privacy, consent, and human review gates are satisfied.

Prohibited functions:

- policing or detention;
- forced entry;
- weapons deployment;
- harassment or intimidation;
- collecting sensitive data without a defined need and consent;
- impersonating emergency personnel;
- declaring legal guilt, fraud, or official misconduct without verified evidence;
- treating silence, absence, or nonresponse as consent.

## Minority Report doctrine

Every county action report should preserve both the prevailing operational decision and the dissenting record.

```text
COUNTY DECISION
+ MAJORITY RATIONALE
+ WOMEN'S AUTHORED OPINIONS
+ MINORITY REPORTS
+ UNANSWERED QUESTIONS
+ UNMET NEEDS
+ EVIDENCE REFERENCES
+ AUTHORITY SOURCE
+ AFTER-ACTION RESPONSE
```

The minority report is not a prediction of crime. It is a protected record of concerns, alternatives, warnings, and evidence that did not control the final decision.

## Grid-down record kit

Each unit should maintain a low-tech kit:

- numbered paper observation forms;
- carbon-copy or duplicate pages;
- county map and facility list;
- pens, permanent markers, tape, envelopes, and tamper-evident seals;
- battery-powered time source and radios where lawful;
- accessibility cards and large-print forms;
- consent and privacy notices;
- emergency contact sheet;
- sequential incident ledger;
- return-to-network hashing instructions.

## Observation record

Each observation should separate:


Rumor and inference must be labeled as such.

## Return-to-network procedure

When connectivity returns:

```text
PAPER / OFFLINE RECORD
→ DUAL-PERSON ENTRY CHECK
→ SCAN OR TRANSCRIBE
→ PRESERVE ORIGINAL
→ HASH ARTIFACT
→ RECORD CUSTODY CHAIN
→ LINK COUNTY + INCIDENT
→ HUMAN PRIVACY REVIEW
→ PUBLISH OR WITHHOLD
→ AFTER-ACTION RECEIPT
```

No original record is overwritten by a cleaned or summarized version.

## Escalation

Immediate danger is routed to lawful emergency services. Infrastructure failures are routed to the responsible utility or agency. Civil-rights, public-records, and governance concerns are recorded separately and reviewed after immediate safety needs.

```text
SAFETY RESPONSE != EVIDENCE ADJUDICATION
```
