## MM proof posture

This study does not treat a political legitimacy judgment as a binary legal result.

```text
LEGALITY_TRACK:
  constitutional_text
  statute
  authorization
  delegation
  court_ruling
  procedural_compliance

LEGITIMACY_TRACK:
  declared_normative_standard
  representation
  transparency
  notice
  reason-giving
  accountability
  correction
  public-consent / democratic-process questions
```

A result may be legally contested while politically legitimate to some observers, legally permitted while considered illegitimate under a declared normative standard, or unresolved on both axes.
