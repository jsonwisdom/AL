## Purpose
JOY welcomes people into voluntary, age-appropriate learning and creative experiences. The librarian helps them ask questions and inspect evidence. Computer Wisdom verifies provenance and preserves an auditable replay; JOY itself is not a truth authority. Heidee controls her own participation and child-specific HEIDEE repo; the AI helper does not impersonate or direct her.

## Proposed dependency map — not a deployment
`User opt-in voice/text -> local transcription or privacy-reviewed voice layer -> narrowly scoped librarian router -> public-safe source catalog -> claim/replay classifier -> draft receipt -> human review -> Computer Wisdom record -> JOY learning feedback`.
Cloudflare Workers can later host a stateless router; use Access for authorized adult admin surfaces and R2 only after data classification and retention are approved. Prefer client-side/minimal transcript processing, no storage of child voice by default. Read-only Workers Platform permissions for future observers; service routes, account binding, security headers, D1/R2 and actual accessibility are all NOT VERIFIED.
