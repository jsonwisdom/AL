# Receipt First Manifesto

Proof over narrative.

A claim is not a product until it has a receipt.
A receipt is not trusted until it can be replayed.
A sale is not clean unless the ledger sees it first.

This store begins with one rule:

No sellable artifact without ledger entry.

The artifact is not hype.
The artifact is the audit trail.

Receipt first.
Anchor second.
Profit third.
