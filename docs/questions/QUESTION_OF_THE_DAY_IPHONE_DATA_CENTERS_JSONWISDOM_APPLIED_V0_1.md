## Question

In the future, what is Jay's iPhone capable of when it has a WiFi connection to data centers?

ALL of them.

## Short Answer

Jay's iPhone becomes an edge command notebook for learning, receipts, replay, agents, verifiers, data work, and machine-speed invention.

It is not powerful because the phone does everything alone.

It is powerful because the phone can safely ask the right machines to do the right bounded work, then bring back receipts.

## The Big Idea

The iPhone is not the factory.

The iPhone is the steering wheel, dashboard, notebook, camera, microphone, verifier, and receipt viewer.

The data centers are the engines.

JSONWisdom Applied is the rule system that keeps the work checkable.
