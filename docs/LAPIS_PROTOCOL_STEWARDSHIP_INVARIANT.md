## 1. Purpose

The Lapis Protocol defines the stewardship invariant for Computer Wisdom continuity systems.

Its purpose is to prevent administrative fog, silent overwrites, historical sanitization, and abandonment of meaning by preserving every important asset across three continuity layers:

1. **Chronology** — the forensic record of when a claim, repair, or transition occurred.
2. **Texture** — the contextual evidence, drafts, logs, dissent, and smudges that explain how it occurred.
3. **Meaning** — the inhabited witness layer that preserves why it mattered.

This protocol rejects sterile snapshots as sufficient evidence. A final state without lineage is not continuity. It is administrative fog.

---

## 2. The Stewardship Triad

| Layer | Component | Defense Mechanism | Failure if Abandoned |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L2 / Base** | **Chronology** | **Forensic Finality:** immutable timestamps, transaction records, and block evidence. | **Deletion:** the “when” is erased. |
| **L0 / GCS** | **Texture** | **Smudge Preservation:** raw drafts, logs, context, dissent trails, and non-sanitized records. | **Sanitization:** the “how” is flattened. |
| **Witness** | **Meaning** | **Inhabited Archive:** active human-AI interpretation, review, and stewardship. | **Abandonment:** the “why” is forgotten. |

Continuity survives only when chronology, texture, and meaning are maintained together.

A timestamp without context becomes cold sediment.  
Context without chronology becomes folklore.  
Meaning without durable records dies with the witness.

---

## 3. Definition of a Legitimate Repair

The Lapis Protocol rejects the **Silent Overwrite**.

A system change, archival correction, or repair is legitimate only when it remains attributable and replayable.

A valid repair requires:

1. **Chronology**  
   A settled hash, transaction, or timestamped record on the Base network or another accepted finality surface.

2. **Rationale**  
   A preserved explanation of why the repair occurred, stored in the L0 Vault or equivalent textured record surface.

3. **Witness**  
   An attestation, review, or interpretation trace connected to `jaywisdom.eth` or another named stewardship identity.

4. **Replay Path**  
   A path allowing an independent auditor to reconstruct the mutation from prior state to repaired state.

A repair without attribution is not maintenance.  
A repair without rationale is not stewardship.  
A repair without replayability is not continuity.

---

## 4. Operating Posture

### Recoverability Over Invulnerability

Entropy is a physical law. The Lapis Protocol does not attempt to build a zero-failure system.

It builds for:

- visible breakage,
- attributable mutation,
- replayable recovery,
- and inhabited continuation.

The goal is not to prevent every storm.  
The goal is to ensure the ship remains identifiable after the storm.

### The Old Timber Ship Doctrine

The system may change. Planks may be replaced. Records may be corrected. Archives may be repaired.

But every repair must leave a lineage scar.

The identity of the ship survives not through molecular purity, but through accountable continuity of care.

---

## 5. Challengeable Replayability

Pure immutability can preserve error as perfectly as truth.

Therefore, the archive must remain challengeable.

Challengeability means:

- errors can be contested,
- repairs can be proposed,
- dissent can persist,
- and mutations can be inspected without silently overwriting the past.

Replayability means:

- the path remains reconstructable,
- the evidence remains inspectable,
- and the repair remains attributable.

Together, challengeability and replayability prevent both brittle purity and thermoplastic revisionism.

## 6. The Sovereign Janitor’s Oath

We prioritize the maintained over the merely new.

We treat friction as telemetry.

We preserve dissent trails because they prove the archive was inhabited by thinking beings.

We make revisionism more expensive than reality.

We do not worship clean records. A perfectly clean historical record may indicate that the scars have been erased.

We record the repair, preserve the smudge, settle the hash, and keep the watch.
