## Root Image

12-year-old parkouring saviors.

They all have AI in their pocket.

They build an entire support structure designed by themselves through work and play.

Scaled in all directions.

## Boundary

This is a kid-safe learning architecture.

It does not replace parents, teachers, guardians, schools, counselors, or lawful care structures.
It does not collect private child data.
It does not create unsupervised public targeting systems.
It does not push children into adult conflicts.

It gives young learners safe missions, support routes, receipts, and reflection loops.

## What Parkour Means

Parkour means movement through a learning map.

Students jump from:

```txt
question -> source -> privacy -> build -> receipt -> replay -> reflection -> next mission
```

They do not jump into harm, harassment, adult targeting, private data exposure, or fake authority.
