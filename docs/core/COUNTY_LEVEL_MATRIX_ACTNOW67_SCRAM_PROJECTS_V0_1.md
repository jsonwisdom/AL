## County-Level Learning Map

County level means local context, not jurisdictional control.

Allowed county-level inputs:

- school lunch menus
- public meeting notices
- library calendars
- sports schedules
- public budgets
- retail prices
- coupon terms
- weather-safe planning
- public documents
- local history
- family learning routes
- community events

Forbidden county-level uses:

- targeting officials or private people
- collecting child data
- political mobilization
- impersonating schools or agencies
- claiming official county authority
- unsafe in-person missions
