## Purpose

Middle School Matrix Games helps students move from simple checking into teamwork, projects, replay, digital responsibility, and machine-speed creativity.

This layer is about:

- confidence
- teamwork
- digital literacy
- privacy
- receipts
- replay
- creativity
- invention
- safe online movement

## Root Rule

```txt
Fast minds still need checked reality.
```

## Core Learning Tracks

### Track 1: Source Intelligence

Students learn:

- source checking
- misinformation spotting
- citation basics
- evidence comparison
- signal vs noise

Kid rule:

```txt
Loud does not mean true.
```

---

### Track 2: Privacy and Identity

Students learn:

- passwords
- consent
- digital footprints
- oversharing risks
- public vs private data
- safe collaboration

Kid rule:

```txt
Protect your keys and your future self.
```

---

### Track 3: Receipts and Replay

Students learn:

- receipts as proof
- hashes as fingerprints
- replay as safe checking
- why replay never touches real APIs
- why logs are not enough

Kid rule:

```txt
If it matters, keep the proof.
```

---

### Track 4: Repo Parkour

Students learn:

- navigating repos
- reading folders as rooms
- understanding routes
- tracing dependencies
- documenting work
- building small tools

Kid rule:

```txt
Move room by room.
```

---

### Track 5: Machine-Speed Creativity

Students learn:

- brainstorming
- rapid iteration
- safe AI usage
- collaboration
- design thinking
- invention loops

Kid rule:

```txt
Build fast. Check faster.
```

---

## Team Missions

Students complete missions such as:

- build a receipt card
- create a replay demo
- make a privacy guide
- design a verifier checklist
- create a room in repo parkour
- explain hashes to younger students
- teach Rule 1 to parents
