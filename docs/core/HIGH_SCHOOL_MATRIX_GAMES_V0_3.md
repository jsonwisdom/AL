## Purpose

High School is Jay's favorite layer because this is when learners begin to discover purpose, discipline, identity, talent, humor, work, money, technology, service, and future trajectory.

This is also when institutions begin to notice talent.

The lesson is not fear.

The lesson is discernment.

## Root Frame

```txt
Middle School: Build carefully. Verify together.
High School: Discover purpose. Choose your path. Keep receipts.
```

## High School Reality

High school students begin encountering:

- recruiters
- colleges
- employers
- creators
- platforms
- agencies
- algorithms
- debt offers
- career tracks
- public identity
- digital footprints
- social pressure
- adult systems

The goal is to teach them how to inspect opportunities without being consumed by them.

## Core Rule

```txt
If someone wants your talent, ask what they want, what they offer, what they collect, and what proof exists.
```

## Opportunity Literacy

Students learn to inspect:

```json
{
  "opportunity_questions": [
    "Who is offering this?",
    "What do they want from me?",
    "What do I get?",
    "What data do they collect?",
    "What rights do I keep?",
    "What obligations do I accept?",
    "Can I leave?",
    "Is there a receipt?"
  ]
}
```

## College and Career Discernment

College can be useful.
College can be expensive.
Career paths can be powerful.
Recruiters can open doors.
Institutions can also shape people in ways they do not notice.

Jay's AL teaches students to inspect systems before joining them.

```txt
Do not get programmed by default.
Choose your inputs.
Check the terms.
Protect your mind.
Build your own receipts.
```

## Agency / Recruiter Literacy

If a government agency, company, lab, university, or platform notices your talent, the student should not panic or worship it.

They should ask:

```json
{
  "recruiter_literacy": [
    "what_is_the_mission",
    "what_is_the_contract",
    "what_is_the_training_path",
    "what_are_the_ethics",
    "what_are_the_constraints",
    "what_rights_do_i_keep",
    "what_records_exist",
    "who_can_i_talk_to_before_deciding"
  ]
}
```

## High School Missions

Students may complete missions such as:

- audit an opportunity letter
- compare two college paths
- build a public portfolio page
- create a receipt-backed resume artifact
- explain privacy rights in plain language
- design a comedy bit that teaches source checking
- create a verifier checklist for AI outputs
- map a skill into a career route
- build one useful tool and document it
