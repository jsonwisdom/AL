## Scope

Chapter 13 governs how Minnesota government entities collect, classify, store, access, and share government data.

Citizen-facing topics include:

- public access to government data,
- data classifications,
- rights of individuals who are the subject of government data,
- agency obligations,
- remedies and penalties,
- commissioner advisory opinions,
- personnel data,
- law enforcement data,
- educational data,
- medical data,
- welfare data,
- social security number protections,
- security breach notification,
- Safe at Home participant data.

| Question | Minnesota — Chapter 13 | Alabama — Open Records Act |
|---|---|---|
| Primary public-records route | Revisor §§ 13.03 and 13.04 | Code §§ 36-12-40 through 36-12-46 |
| Who may request | § 13.03 refers to a **person** seeking public data; § 13.04 covers the **individual who is the data subject** | § 36-12-40 establishes a **resident** right, subject to exceptions; § 36-12-44 discusses requests by nonresidents |
| Standard public-record response | Appropriate and prompt under § 13.03; **not** an across-the-board ten-day deadline | § 36-12-44: generally acknowledge a proper standard request within **10 business days**, then provide a substantive response within **15 business days** after acknowledgment; extensions, fees, and exceptions apply |
| Data-subject response | § 13.04, subd. 3: immediately if possible, or within ten days excluding weekends/legal holidays | No equivalent data-subject deadline is asserted by this index; research separately before comparing |
| Accuracy challenge | § 13.04, subd. 4: written challenge, typically 30-day determination | No equivalent procedure asserted by this index |
| Evidence status | Official law is a source for research, **not** proof that an agency complied or violated the law | Same; law and case evidence must remain separate |
