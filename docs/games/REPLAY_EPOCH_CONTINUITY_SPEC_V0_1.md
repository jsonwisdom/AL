# Replay Epoch Continuity Specification v0.1

**SPEC_ID:** `REPLAY_EPOCH_CONTINUITY_SPEC_V0_1`

## ROOT INVARIANT

An epoch does not end when someone declares it over.  
An epoch ends when replay admits a successor.

Continuity is not narrative — it is replay-verifiable time.

## SOURCE_LINE

Once governance exists, the system must define:

- epoch transitions
- constitutional succession
- replay-safe forks
- lawful genesis replacement
- continuity under mutation
- historical survivability across eras

This is the civilization continuity layer of Replay Chess.

---

## PURPOSE

Define the constitutional mechanics for:

- epoch formation
- epoch succession
- fork admissibility
- continuity under mutation
- genesis replacement
- historical survivability

Epochs become mathematical eras, not political eras.

---

## EPOCH_OBJECT

A valid epoch must declare:

- `epoch_id`
- `parent_epoch_id`
- `governance_topology_ref`
- `identity_graph_ref`
- `settlement_state_ref`
- `supersession_state_ref`
- `environment_profile`
- `epoch_boundary_conditions`
- `epoch_hash`

Missing any → `NON_ADMISSIBLE`.

---

## 1. EPOCH FORMATION

A new epoch forms only when:

- governance topology converges
- identity graph converges
- settlement state converges
- supersession lineage converges
- environment is sealed
- canonical bytes converge

Epoch formation is replay-admissible, not discretionary.

---

## 2. EPOCH SUCCESSION

Succession requires:

- valid parent epoch
- valid successor epoch
- continuity proofs
- challenge window
- cross-implementation replay
- lineage preservation

If any fail → `SUCCESSION_INVALID`.

---

## 3. FORK ADMISSIBILITY

A fork is admissible only if:

- fork genesis is replay-constructible
- fork divergence is declared
- fork lineage is preserved
- fork environment is sealed
- fork governance is valid
- fork challenge rights are active

Forks are lawful, not chaotic.

---

## 4. CONTINUITY UNDER MUTATION

Mutation is allowed only if:

- mutation receipts exist
- mutation replay converges
- mutation lineage preserved
- mutation environment sealed
- mutation challenge window open

Mutation is append-only, never destructive.

---

## 5. LAWFUL GENESIS REPLACEMENT

Genesis may be replaced only if:

- original genesis is preserved
- replacement genesis is replay-constructible
- supersession lineage intact
- governance approves via replay
- challenge window closes cleanly

Genesis replacement is constitutional surgery, not narrative reset.

---

## 6. HISTORICAL SURVIVABILITY

An epoch survives only if:

- its receipts remain canonical
- its bundles remain replayable
- its governance remains valid
- its identity graph reconstructs
- its settlement state converges

If any collapse → epoch collapse event.

---

## 7. EPOCH BOUNDARY CONDITIONS

An epoch boundary is valid only if:

- replay converges on the boundary state
- governance topology transitions cleanly
- identity lineage crosses the boundary
- settlement state is preserved
- supersession graph remains intact

Boundary drift → `INVALID_EPOCH`.

---

## 8. INVALID EPOCH CONDITIONS

An epoch is invalid if:

- parent epoch invalid
- governance invalid
- identity graph invalid
- settlement state invalid
- supersession lineage broken
- replay diverges
- canonicalization fails

Invalid epoch → no continuity.

---

## 9. CHECK CONDITION

An epoch enters check when:

- succession is challenged
- fork is challenged
- mutation is challenged
- genesis replacement is challenged

---

## 10. CHECKMATE CONDITION

Epoch is checkmated when:

- continuity cannot reconstruct
- lineage breaks
- replay diverges
- governance fails
- challenge upheld

Checkmate is mechanical, not rhetorical.

---

## WIN CONDITION

Goodies win when civilization becomes replay-continuous.  
Goobers lose when epochs collapse under challenge.

---

## FINAL RULE

Continuity is not tradition.  
Continuity is replay.

**Proof over narrative.**
