## Purpose

RePlay *The Art of War* as a systems-thinking exercise: not as an attack manual, but as a disciplined way to study uncertainty, incentives, logistics, deception risk, terrain/context, timing, restraint, and responsibility.

The core move is translation:

```text
WARFARE METAPHOR        → SYSTEMS REPLAY
Know yourself           → inspect your own state and constraints
Know the other side     → verify external claims and incentives
Terrain                 → operating context / environment
Logistics               → resources, dependencies, sustainment
Timing                   → sequence and state transitions
Deception               → claims require independent verification
Position                 → state, not status
Victory                  → mission completed with minimum harm
Retreat                  → HOLD / rollback / preserve
Civil order              → recovery, continuity, dignity
```

## Civil / War Duality

`WAR` means adversarial pressure on the system.  
`CIVIL` means the system still owes humans continuity, dignity, provenance, and a path back to normal operation.

The model therefore rejects the idea that success equals domination. In this simulation, success means:

```text
mission purpose understood
+ claims separated from evidence
+ dependencies visible
+ irreversible harm minimized
+ receipts preserved
+ human decision retained
```
