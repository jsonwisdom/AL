# Constitutional Replay Economy v0.1

Status: BUSINESS_MODEL_DRAFT
Operational status: NON_BINDING_COMMERCIAL_FRAMEWORK

## Core Principle

Revenue cannot buy attention, speech, interpretation, verdicts, target inclusion, target removal, dashboard semantics, RAP unlocks, or mainnet authority.

Revenue may buy:

- scale
- speed
- uptime
- support
- exports
- integrations
- service-level guarantees
- reviewer labor for governed PR processes

The constitution does not change for customers.

## What the Machine Produces

The machine produces attested observations of public-record procedural state.

The public 404 surface emits only:

```json
[
  "FOUND",
  "NOT_FOUND",
  "VERSION_DRIFT",
  "CRAWLER_BLOCKED"
]
```

No customer may purchase alternative semantics.

## One-Paragraph Pitch

Constitutional Replay is public-record observability infrastructure. We monitor public URLs and emit receipts that say only `FOUND`, `NOT_FOUND`, `VERSION_DRIFT`, or `CRAWLER_BLOCKED`. We cannot call something suspicious, assign risk, or sell interpretation because the repo rejects those semantics in tests and runtime guards. Customers can pay for speed, uptime, exports, alerts, and integrations. Customers cannot pay us to look away, look harder, change verdicts, or add narrative labels. The rules that bind us are public, tested in CI, and enforced at runtime.

## Non-Claims

- customer legitimacy

- mainnet activation
- RAP activation
- proof that a public record is complete

It defines only membrane-safe commercial access to a constitutionally bounded public-record replay machine.
