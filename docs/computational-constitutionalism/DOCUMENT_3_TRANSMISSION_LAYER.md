---

# The Five Layers

## 1. Transport

Moves bytes.
Does not interpret them.

## 2. Replay

Reconstructs state deterministically.
Does not negotiate outcomes.

## 3. Witness

Validates convergence across independent observers.
Does not manufacture authority.

## 4. Governance

Evolves rules forward through replayable amendments.
Does not rewrite the past.

## 5. Narrative

Explains meaning to humans.
Does not override proof.

---

# Constitutional Ordering

No layer may override the layer beneath it.

Violation of this rule collapses the system back into theater.

---

# Memory Commandments

- Truth is what survives replay.
- No layer may override the layer beneath it.
- Transport moves bytes; it does not interpret them.
- Replay reconstructs state; it does not negotiate it.
- Witnesses verify convergence; they do not manufacture authority.
- Governance may evolve rules forward; it may not rewrite the past.
- Narrative may explain proof; it may not replace proof.
- A broken proof is not a story opportunity.
- If the root cannot be reproduced, the claim is not sovereign.
- When certainty degrades, the system must say so.

---

# Ritual of Verification

1. Receive the event log.
2. Receive the canonicalization manifest.
3. Resolve the manifest hash.
4. Replay the event stream.
5. Compute the root.
6. Compare roots with independent witnesses.
7. Publish verdict:
   - PASS
   - FAIL
   - DEGRADED

Verification is a civic act.

---

# Honest Degradation

A truthful system must declare:
- what it can still prove
- what it can no longer prove
- what confidence has degraded
- what witnesses are missing
- what replay surfaces have diverged

False certainty is constitutional corruption.

---

# Observer Diversity

Apparent consensus is not sufficient.

Multiple copies of the same implementation do not constitute independent verification.

The system should encourage:
- runtime diversity
- implementation diversity
- geographic diversity
- governance diversity
- human + machine witness diversity

Monoculture convergence is fragile convergence.

---

# Survivability Requirement

The system must remain replayable if:
- the company disappears
- the servers disappear
- the blockchain disappears
- the UI disappears
- the maintainers disappear

The event log + manifest + verifier specification must be sufficient for continuity.

---

# Rebuild Requirement

A future observer must be able to reconstruct the verifier from:
- the event stream
- the canonicalization manifest
- the invariant rules
- the replay specification

No hidden authority may be required.

---

# Final Thesis

A civilization that cannot replay its memory must trust whoever controls the story.

The purpose of computational constitutionalism is not perfect truth.

It is durable, inspectable, replayable continuity under entropy.
