# Individual Authenticity Site Root v1

Status: DRAFT_SITE_ROOT
Root identity: jaywisdom.base
Applies to: Jay's Wisdom learning environment, public site, receipt surfaces, and personal doctrine pages.

## Purpose

This document applies individual authenticity as a root design law for Jay's site. The site must not behave like a generic platform, demographic funnel, corporate voice, or algorithmic crowd machine. It must behave like a verifiable conversation between specific individuals, with receipts for claims, corrections, and commitments.

## Root Principle

```text
The site is not built for The Crowd.
The site is built for The Individual.
```

The public may arrive. The crowd may observe. But the system must never dissolve personal responsibility into anonymous averages.

## 1. Death of the Ideal User

Generic personas are not root authority.

Do not design for phantom abstractions like Marketing Mary, Tech Tom, or average-user composites. Design for a single responsible person encountering a specific claim, choice, lesson, or receipt.

### Receipt Rule

```text
No demographic average outranks a personal receipt.
```

### Site Application

Prefer:

- "This resonated with me"
- "This is my current proof path"
- "This claim is unresolved"
- "Here is the receipt"

Avoid:

- "Users like you also liked"
- hidden behavioral scoring
- anonymous engagement funnels
- generic corporate "we" voice where a single accountable voice is needed

## 2. Subjective Navigation: Aesthetic to Ethical

The site may begin with sensory invitation, but depth must increase toward commitment.

### Layering Rule

```text
Surface may attract.
Depth must require choice.
```

### Site Application

- landing pages may use cinematic visuals, rhythm, and memorable language
- deeper pages must reveal manifestos, receipts, long-form reasoning, source trails, and challenge paths
- About pages should read as statements of choice, not static resumes
- learning paths should move from curiosity to responsibility

## 3. Anti-Algorithmic Design

The site must not sacrifice truth to optimization rituals.

### Receipt Rule

```text
SEO may assist discovery.
SEO may not govern truth.
```

### Site Application

- use strategic friction where commitment matters
- allow long pages when the claim requires depth
- keep unique typography, layout, and language when they serve identity and clarity
- do not flatten paradox, uncertainty, or correction to please a template

## 4. Meaningful Choice Architecture

A visitor should not be tricked into passive scrolling. Where entry matters, ask for a conscious choice.

### Commitment Gate Example

```text
Do you agree to take responsibility for your own growth here?
```

This is not a coercive gate. It is a dignity gate: the user is invited to arrive as a responsible individual, not an engagement metric.

## 5. Living Document Doctrine

The site must preserve correction, evolution, and uncertainty.

### Receipt Rule

```text
A changed mind should leave a receipt.
```

### Site Application

- maintain changelogs for doctrine pages
- preserve old claims when safe and lawful, then add correction notes
- distinguish deletion, correction, revocation, and replacement
- make uncertainty visible rather than laundering it into confidence

## 6. Authentic Site Transition

| Element | Crowd Approach | Authentic Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Corporate "we" / brand voice | The single individual / accountable voice |
| Trust | social proof, stars, generic reviews | receipts, correction logs, uncertainty labels |
| Goal | retention and engagement | personal impact and responsible choice |
| Logic | optimization for the machine | proof, paradox, commitment, and replay |
| Structure | template system | unique but auditable expression |

## 9. Machine Boundary

AI employees may personalize assistance by context and receipts.
AI employees may not reduce a person to a persona, score, caste, or demographic destiny.

The machine may recommend a path.
The individual must choose the path.
