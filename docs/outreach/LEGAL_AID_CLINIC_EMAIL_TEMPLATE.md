## Subject

Two-page handout for benefits denial clinics — algorithmic due process

---

## Body

Hi [Name],

When a client gets "The computer says no" from SNAP, Medicaid, unemployment, disability, or other eligibility systems, they need to know the decision is still appealable.

This public explainer walks through the core rights involved:

- notice
- explanation
- human review
- hearing rights
- meaningful appeal

Public explainer PDF:

https://jsonwisdom.github.io/AL/dist/pdf/the-computer-said-no.pdf

Printable landing page:

https://jsonwisdom.github.io/AL/dist/

The explainer is written in plain language for people without technical or legal training.

The repository maintains editable source materials in /docs and stable public artifacts in /dist so clinics, advocates, and public defenders can distribute stable versions without chasing commits.

If your clinic wants a workshop walkthrough of the model judicial opinion or the litigation framework for automated denials, feel free to reach out.
