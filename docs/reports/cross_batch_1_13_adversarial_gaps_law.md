## Universal System Law

### ALMS Law of Adversarial Gaps

> When a system's stated goal conflicts with a legal incentive structure, the system will optimize toward the incentive, not the goal. The gap between mandate and mechanism becomes the operating logic.

Formalized:

```text
mandate != incentive -> gap
response < stress -> gap persists
if gap is legally tolerated -> actors optimize through it
```

Extended model:

```text
control -> stress -> response -> gap -> terminal_behavior -> incentive
```

## Surgical Takeaway

```text
The stated goal tells you what the system claims to serve.
The incentive surface tells you what the system actually serves.
The gap between them is where institutional truth appears.
```

ALMS does not ask whether a system is good or bad.

It asks:

> What does the system reward after the gap is exposed?
