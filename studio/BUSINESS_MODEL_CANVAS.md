# Business Model Canvas — Jay's Secret Artifacts Stack

## Value Proposition
Verifiable state-level infrastructure that produces proof artifacts, learning
tools, and public accountability dashboards — monetized as kits, subscriptions,
and premium services.

## Customer Segments
- **State Governments** — Deployment, dashboards, legal wrappers
- **Cities & Municipalities** — Node kits, courtesy notices, regional subscriptions
- **Universities & Civic Institutes** — Learning kits, playbooks, scenario drills
- **Campaigns & Advocacy Groups** — Signal media kits, public education assets
- **Journalism & Oversight** — Dashboard access, match/mismatch logs, latency scorecards
