 1   Enrolled, An Act,
 2
 3         To make appropriations for the ordinary expenses of the

 4   executive, legislative, and judicial agencies of the State,
 5   for other functions of government, for debt service, and for

 6   capital outlay for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026.
 7   BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:

 8         Section 1. The monies in Section 3 are appropriated
 9   from the named funds for the 2026 fiscal year to the state

10   agencies indicated, as the amounts to be used to pay the
11   expenditures of the named agencies, and are in lieu of all

12   monies appropriated for these purposes in other sections of
13   the Alabama Statutes.

14         For the purpose of this act, the amounts herein for
15   expenditures are listed by programmatic area and the totals

16   for all programs are shown by the source of funds. It is
17   intended that only the herein named funds be appropriated in
18   the amounts specified to the named agencies and that the

19   following definitions shall be applicable.
20         "Total" shall mean the aggregate total appropriation of

21   all funding sources.
22         "Program" shall mean specific governmental services

23   required to achieve a specific objective. A program shall be
24   directed to meeting the need of an identified clientele, or

25   group of recipients or beneficiaries, and shall be expended
26   only for such purposes.

27         "Capital Outlay" shall mean expenditures which result




                                   Page 1
                            HB186 Enrolled

28   in the acquisition and/or addition to items, such as land or
29   buildings, which have an appreciable and calculable period of
30   usefulness in excess of one year, and shall be expended only

31   for such purposes.
32         "Debt Service" shall mean an expenditure for the

33   payment of interest and principal on bonded debt obligations
34   of the State, and shall be expended only for such purposes.

35         "Federal and Local Funds" shall mean all gifts, grants,
36   contributions, or entitlements, including grants by the

37   Congress of the United States, municipalities or counties.
38         Section 2. (a) There is hereby appropriated the amounts

39   set forth herein for the ordinary expenses of the executive,
40   legislative, and judicial agencies of the State, for other

41   functions of government, for debt service, and for capital
42   outlay for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, to be

43   paid out of any monies hereinafter specified, from such other
44   funds and accounts as may be designated, or so much thereof as
45   may be necessary, and the total amount to be expended for the

46   items for which the appropriation is herein made shall not
47   exceed the amount provided therefor, except as provided in the

48   Budget Management Act of 1976, Sections 41-19-1 through
49   41-19-12, Code of Alabama 1975. Provided, however, that

50   regardless of the ending date of any pay period which has been
51   or may be established by the Legislature for the payment of

52   salaries of state employees, the entire payment due shall be
53   made from the fiscal year's appropriation in which the pay

54   date falls.
