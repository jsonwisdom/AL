# FAF Epoch 03 Caselaw Classification Rule

Status: `DECLARED`

## Classification

The first successful Epoch 03 convergence SHALL be classified as:

```text
EPOCH_03_ALPHA_CASELAW_001
```

## Scope

This is procedural caselaw, not semantic authority.

It establishes precedent that:

- the Alpha verification process can bind capsule, runtime, and fixtures;
- the replay matrix can produce deterministic convergence;
- dual human receipts can agree on Merkle outputs;
- CI can remain a witness without becoming an oracle;
- the anchor gate can remain locked until convergence evidence exists.

It does **not** establish that the underlying doctrine is semantically true, politically correct, morally complete, or externally authoritative.

## Refusal Caselaw

A refusal event may also produce caselaw when it demonstrates lawful halt behavior under ALMS refusal policy.

Examples:

```text
EPOCH_03_REFUSAL_CASELAW_001_ALMS_03
EPOCH_03_REFUSAL_CASELAW_002_ALMS_02
```

## Boundary

Successful convergence proves that the machine followed its own rules.

Refusal proves that the machine refused to violate its own rules.

Neither turns the chain into a judge.

```text
Replay verifies.
Chain remembers.
```
