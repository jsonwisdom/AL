Status: CLAIM_WITNESS_ONLY

## Default Rule

No child identity is public by default.

Child identities, subnames, derived identifiers, and protected dependent surfaces are not discoverable through ENS, JOY, ALMS, or public replay surfaces unless an explicit receipt permits limited visibility.

## Emergency Visibility Exception

Emergency visibility may occur only when all conditions are met:

1. An explicit receipt exists.
2. The emergency context is declared.
3. EAS witnesses the claim in CLAIM_WITNESS_ONLY form.
4. No custody mutation occurs.
5. No legal, medical, governmental, or emergency authority is asserted.
6. The membrane remains HOLDS.

## What This Does Not Allow

- No public child listing by default.
- No custody transfer.
- No permanent exposure.
- No silent promotion.
- No authority claim.
