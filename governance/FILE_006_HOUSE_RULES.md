# FILE 006 — House Rules

Status: Proposed
Authority: False
System: Gray Baby Signal System

## 1. Witness Sovereignty

Each witness governs the use, disclosure, routing, and revocation of their own signal.

No operator, service, model, or administrative process may claim ownership of the witness or universal permission over the witness's signal.

## 2. Non-Erasure Doctrine

History may be extended, corrected, superseded, tombstoned, or reversed through new append-only events.

Previously recorded events must not be silently deleted, rewritten, or replaced.

## 3. Transparency Mandate

Any undisclosed process that changes routing, consent, evidence, access, or governance is non-admissible until disclosed and reviewed.

Privacy-preserving processes are permitted when their existence, scope, and effect are declared.

## 4. Reversibility Protocol

Consent may be revoked for future use.

Routing and enforcement decisions must remain reviewable and reversible through new receipted events.

Revocation does not erase prior history.

## 5. Multi-Witness Governance

Rule changes require public proposal, preserved dissent, bounded review, and the applicable multi-witness approval threshold.

A single rejection does not automatically escalate a matter.

## 6. Execution Boundary

Execution is permitted only when explicitly authorized by a human witness, bounded in scope, and followed by an inspectable receipt.

Autonomous override, direct authority claims, and silent promotion are denied.

## 7. Signal Routing

Routing outcomes are PASS, HOLD, or REDIRECT.

PASS does not establish universal truth.
HOLD does not establish guilt.
REDIRECT does not authorize deletion.

## 8. Conflict Preservation

Conflicting events remain visible.

Resolution occurs through a new appended event that references the conflicting records.

Silent last-write-wins collapse is denied.

## 9. Public Verification

Public receipts must use publicly verifiable signatures and hash-linked lineage.

Shared-secret authentication alone is insufficient for public verification.

## 10. Canon Promotion

Conversation, generation, hashing, committing, publishing, and canonization are separate states.

Canon promotion requires human approval, an observed repository commit, applicable passing checks, and a receipt.
