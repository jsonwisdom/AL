## 3. Civic Mass

Civic mass represents the amount of admitted, traceable support available to a simulated node.

```text
M = sum(admitted_source_anchor_weight)
```

Rules:

- Unverified claims add zero admitted mass.
- Duplicate sources may not be counted as independent mass.
- Hash equality establishes integrity only.
- Authority weight must remain distinct from verification confidence.
- No mass enters the core docket while Gate 1 is blocked.

Current state:

```text
ADMITTED_SOURCE_ANCHORS = 0
CIVIC_MASS              = 0
```

## 4. Applied Force

Applied force is a bounded student action using declared procedure.

Eligible actions include:

```text
STUDY
ISSUE_FRAME
ARGUMENT
COUNTERARGUMENT
EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE
JURISDICTION_REVIEW
SELF_EXAMINATION
JUDICIAL_REVIEW
APPEAL
```

A valid force event must identify:

- actor,
- target node,
- Units of Attention spent,
- rule invoked,
- evidence references,
- intended simulation effect,
- resulting receipt.

Random movement, unsupported assertion, or outrage without declared procedure creates no valid force.

## 5. Energy Budget

```text
INITIAL_UA = 64
```

Units of Attention are a pedagogical resource budget. They do not represent money, intelligence, moral worth, or neurological capacity.

Energy may be spent on movement, study, verification exercises, argument, review, and appeal. Replenishment occurs only through declared session boundaries or earned pedagogical actions such as training and successful knowledge transfer.

```text
UA_SPENT > UA_AVAILABLE -> ACTION_DENIED
```

Verified real-world sources do not automatically become game energy. They must pass the separate Historical Research Gate and external admission authority.

## 6. Friction

Friction represents declared obstacles to reliable procedural movement:

```text
CONTESTED_GAP
DUPLICATE_EVIDENCE
BROKEN_CUSTODY
JURISDICTION_UNCERTAINTY
CONFLICTING_RULE
MISSING_APPEAL_PATH
UNRESOLVED_PROVENANCE
```

A learner may reduce simulated friction by clarifying, distinguishing, preserving a fork, producing a corrective receipt, or remanding for evidence.

A gap may be marked `RESOLVED_IN_SIMULATION`; it may not be called historically settled.

## 7. Load Capacity

Load capacity represents the number and complexity of disputes, forks, appeals, and evidentiary failures the simulation can preserve without deleting history or inventing resolution.

Capacity requires:

- append-only receipts,
- preserved dissents,
- explicit appeal paths,
- redundancy without false independence,
- succession rules,
- recovery from failed nodes,
- bounded session resources.

```text
LOAD > VERIFIED_CAPACITY -> FAIL_CLOSED
```

Fail-closed results include:

```text
REMAND_FOR_EVIDENCE
INDETERMINATE
SESSION_SUSPENDED
CAPACITY_EXCEEDED
```

## 8. Equilibrium

The SELF and JUSTICE axes form a pedagogical feedback loop.

```text
SELF     = bias recognition, discipline, and responsibility
JUSTICE  = procedure, evidence boundaries, review, and remedy
```

Diagnostic states:

```text
S >> J -> EGO_RISK
J >> S -> MECHANICAL_JUSTICE_RISK
S ~= J -> BALANCED_REVIEW_CANDIDATE
```

These states trigger reflection or scenario adjustment. They do not diagnose a person or determine fitness, character, or legal capacity.

## 9. Momentum

```text
P = participation * directional_coherence * continuity
```

Where:

- participation = valid procedural nodes visited,
- directional coherence = consistency between issue, evidence, argument, and requested effect,
- continuity = valid linkage to prior simulation receipts.

Momentum cannot substitute for verification or authorization.

```text
HIGH_MOMENTUM + NO_AUTHORITY = NO_AUTHORITY
```

## 10. Growth Cycle

```text
OBSERVE
-> MEASURE
-> ORGANIZE
-> APPLY_FORCE
-> RECORD_EFFECT
-> CORRECT_ERROR
-> EXPAND_CAPACITY
-> REPEAT
```

Game mapping:

1. **Observe:** Load a `PEDAGOGICAL_ONLY` scenario.
2. **Measure:** Inspect authority claims, gate states, integrity, provenance, and available UA.
3. **Organize:** Select routes, roles, evidence tokens, and procedural sequence.
4. **Apply force:** Submit argument and counterargument under bounded rules.
5. **Record effect:** Emit append-only receipts and an educational outcome.
6. **Correct error:** Perform SELF-axis and JUSTICE-axis review.
7. **Expand capacity:** Add a reusable educational reference node where permitted.
8. **Repeat:** Load the next fictional or independently admitted exercise.
