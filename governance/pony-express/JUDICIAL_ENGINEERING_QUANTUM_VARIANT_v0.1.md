**Authority:** false

## Purpose

Introduce quantum-inspired uncertainty, linked evidence, observation cost, and decoherence mechanics into the Civic War board-game engine without allowing gameplay to manufacture historical truth, legal validity, or public authority.

This is metaphorical game design. It is not a claim that courts, evidence, votes, or constitutional authority literally operate according to quantum mechanics.

## Judicial Engineering Principle

Judicial engineering is the design of procedures that separate:

```text
CLAIM
EVIDENCE
CUSTODY
JURISDICTION
REVIEW
DECISION
REMEDY
APPEAL
```

A game mechanic may alter uncertainty, cost, routing, or player incentives. It may not collapse an unresolved historical or legal question into authoritative truth.

## Quantum Mechanics

### 1. Superposition Claims

A claim may occupy two provisional game states until the reveal phase:

```text
SUPPORTED | UNSUPPORTED
```

Players commit votes or challenges blind. Reveal resolves only the game state.

```text
SUPERPOSITION_RESOLUTION != HISTORICAL_ADJUDICATION
```

### 2. Entangled Evidence

Two evidence chains may share an integrity dependency through a declared entanglement pair.

If either chain fails an integrity check, both become `CONTAMINATED_PENDING_REVIEW`.

Entanglement does not prove common authorship, common truth, or causal connection.

### 3. Observer Effect

Verification has a game cost. Each observation may change the Civic Integrity Score by the configured amount to model delay, expense, institutional friction, or the value of scrutiny.

The observer effect cannot alter source bytes or retroactively validate a claim.

### 4. Quantum Votes

Votes may be represented as spin states:

```text
UP | DOWN | UNOBSERVED
```

Players may distribute a bounded vote budget across provisional outcomes. Final probability weights must sum to `1.0`.

Credits are game resources only and do not represent civic rights, actual votes, or legal standing.

### 5. Judicial Tunneling

A qualified player may spend credits to bypass a **route obstacle** and reach an otherwise inaccessible review node.

Tunneling may not bypass:

```text
GATE_1
SOURCE_BYTE_REQUIREMENTS
PROVENANCE_REQUIREMENTS
JURISDICTION
DUE_PROCESS
APPEAL_RIGHTS
AUTHORITY_GATES
```

A tunneled claim is marked:

```text
SIMULATED_ROUTE_ONLY
AUTHORITY = FALSE
ADMISSIBILITY = FALSE
```

This mechanic accelerates exploration; it never creates authority.

### 6. Decoherence

If unresolved disagreement exceeds the configured timer, the round decoheres.

The engine must:

1. stop speculative expansion;
2. preserve all forks;
3. return to the earliest independently verified source available;
4. mark remaining claims `UNRESOLVED`;
5. issue a decoherence receipt.

If no verified source exists, the round terminates fail-closed.


No node may be skipped merely because a quantum mechanic is enabled.
