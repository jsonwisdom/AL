## Sworn-record registry

### H01 — identity and role

Grusch stated that he served 14 years as an intelligence officer, including USAF service at the rank of Major, and most recently at NGA at GS-15, which he described as the civilian level equivalent of a full-bird colonel. He stated he was his agency's co-lead for UAP and trans-medium object analysis, reporting to UAPTF and later AARO.

`STATE = PUBLIC_SWORN_STATEMENT`

### H02 — whistleblower route

Grusch stated that he became a whistleblower through a PPD-19 Urgent Concern filing in May 2022 with the Intelligence Community Inspector General and later reported information to multiple Inspectors General.

`STATE = PUBLIC_SWORN_STATEMENT`
`ICIG_ROUTE = BOUND`

### H03 — basis and multi-decade program allegation

Grusch stated that his testimony was based on information given by credentialed current and former military/intelligence personnel, some of whom shared photography, official documentation, and classified oral testimony. He said he attempted corroboration over four years. He stated he was informed during official duties of a multi-decade UAP crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program and was denied additional read-on access.

`PERSONAL_HARDWARE_OBSERVATION = NOT_ESTABLISHED_BY_THIS_STATEMENT`
`UNDERLYING_PROGRAM_VERIFIED = FALSE`

### H04 — government possession claim

Asked whether he believed the government possessed UAPs, Grusch answered affirmatively and said the basis was interviews with more than 40 witnesses over four years.
