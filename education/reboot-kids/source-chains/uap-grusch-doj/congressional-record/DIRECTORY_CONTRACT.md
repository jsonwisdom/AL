# congressional-record — Directory Contract

Purpose: primary-source congressional hearing records associated with the Grusch UAP source chain, separated from later media and social-relay claims.

## Boundaries

- Official congressional hearing records establish what was said, submitted, or entered into the record; they do not establish that the underlying alleged events occurred.
- Sworn testimony is stronger provenance for attribution than press relay, but `UNDER_OATH != UNDERLYING_EVENT_VERIFIED`.
- Prepared witness statements and the printed hearing transcript are primary congressional sources.
- Third-party transcriptions such as Rev are secondary transcription aids and must not outrank the official record.
- Closed briefings or nonpublic committee interactions must not be silently represented as public hearing testimony.
