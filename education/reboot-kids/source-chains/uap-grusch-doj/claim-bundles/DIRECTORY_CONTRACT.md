# claim-bundles — Directory Contract

Purpose: dated claim-routing bundles for the `uap-grusch-doj` source chain.

## Boundaries

- A claim bundle records what a source or relay says; it does not establish that the underlying event occurred.
- Each material assertion must carry its own evidence state.
- User-supplied social summaries remain distinct from directly captured source media.
- Secondary press corroboration may corroborate that a claim was made, not that the claim is true.
- Official conflict records remain attached when applicable.
