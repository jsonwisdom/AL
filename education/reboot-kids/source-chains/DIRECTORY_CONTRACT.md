# source-chains — Directory Contract

Purpose: evidence-routing directories for Alabama ReBoot Kids examples where claims must remain separated from the records that report, archive, dispute, or relay them.

## Boundaries

- Directory placement does not validate an underlying event.
- A record showing that a person made a claim proves the claim was made, not that the claim is true.
- Press relay is not primary evidence of the underlying event.
- An agency records edge is not an agency validation edge.
- Conflicting official assessments must be preserved rather than silently reconciled.
- Unknown, gap, conflict, and hold states remain valid outputs.
